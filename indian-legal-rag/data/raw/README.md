# Raw QA data

Place the owner-provided `constitution_qa.json`, `ipc_qa.json`, and `crpc_qa.json` files here. Each is expected to be an array of objects with non-empty `question` and `answer` strings. The loader processes files independently; do not assume their schemas are otherwise identical.

These are QA datasets and may not contain complete legal texts. Preserve original inputs and provenance. Source identifiers, versions/amendments, effective dates, URLs, and article/section metadata are retained only when explicitly supplied or unambiguously stated. Do not put fabricated or sample legal records in this directory.
